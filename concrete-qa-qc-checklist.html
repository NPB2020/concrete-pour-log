Concrete QA/QC Checklist

Use this field-documentation checklist to keep the records around a concrete pour organized. Always follow the project specification, approved procedures and responsible QA/QC personnel.

Field-documentation guide. This page is a practical recordkeeping resource, not a project specification, engineering instruction, compliance certification or substitute for your approved QA/QC procedures.
01

Before the pour

Confirm the pour identity and location, planned date, supplier, mix reference, ordered volume and the project-specific records that are expected. Make sure the people recording deliveries and tests know where the information will be kept.

02

During delivery

For each truck, capture the ticket or delivery number, delivered quantity and relevant times. Record concrete temperature and other required delivery observations. Keep the ticket image with the delivery it belongs to.

03

During testing

Record each required field test with its type, result, unit, time, sample or specimen reference and notes. Follow the applicable project test procedure; a digital log organizes the record but does not determine acceptance.

04

Issues and deviations

When something notable occurs, record what happened, when it happened, the delivery or part of the pour involved, and the action taken. Add supporting photos where they make the record clearer.

05

Before closeout

Review the documentation for gaps: deliveries, ticket photos, temperatures, discharge information, tests, issues and general pour photos. Confirm the delivered quantity and truck count against the field record.

06

Handover

Create a clear report that keeps the project context, delivery log, tests, issues, notes and supporting images together. Send it to the people responsible for review and retain it with the project records.
