Good to know

Before your first pour.

Who is Concrete Pour Log for?

Foremen, site supervisors, project engineers, QA/QC staff and concrete contractors who need a focused record of individual concrete pours.

Does it work offline?

The core recording workflow and report generation are designed to work on the iPhone without a site internet connection. Sending a report by email or another online service requires connectivity.

Does it support cubic yards?

Yes. The app supports metric and imperial units, including cubic metres and cubic yards for concrete volume.

Is this a laboratory or compliance system?

No. It documents the information and assessments entered by the user. It does not certify test results or replace project-specific QA procedures.

When can I download it?
