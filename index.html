Research on digital field documentation

Why recording on site is worth considering

A 2018 study of HeadLight, a cloud-based inspection system, covered 31 transportation projects across three US state Departments of Transportation over three months. Inspectors saved an average of 1.59 hours per day, including documentation, administration and travel. The authors reported a 25% productivity gain for the activities monitored.

A 2025 study combining analysis of more than 95,000 Dalux Field production issues with a user survey reported benefits including time savings and improved quality control. It also identified weaknesses in reporting standardisation.

These studies evaluated other systems and workflows, not Concrete Pour Log. They provide industry context, not a measured saving or error-reduction rate for this app. Results depend on the task, workflow and user.

Good to know

Before your first pour.

Who is Concrete Pour Log for?

Foremen, site supervisors, project engineers, QA/QC staff and concrete contractors who need a focused record of individual concrete pours.

Does it work offline?

The core recording workflow and report generation are designed to work on the iPhone without a site internet connection. Sending a report by email or another online service requires connectivity.

Does it support cubic yards?

Yes. The app supports metric and imperial units, including cubic metres and cubic yards for concrete volume.

Is this a laboratory or compliance system?

No. It documents the information and assessments entered by the user. It does not certify test results or replace project-specific QA procedures.

What is the difference between annual and lifetime?

Both options unlock Pro with unlimited completed pour reports. Annual costs $19.95 USD per year and renews unless cancelled. Lifetime costs $29.95 USD as a one-time purchase, with no recurring subscription.
